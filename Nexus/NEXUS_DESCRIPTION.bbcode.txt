[b]Party-Up for Kingdom .Inc[/b]

Build the party you actually want. Party-Up adds a Party Node that gathers an exact lineup of up to five adventurers, then sends everyone together when the receiving node has room.

[b]Choose the lineup[/b]
Use the gear popup to set Adventurer, Fighter, Rogue, Mage and Cleric counts. The total cannot exceed five. For 3 Fighters, 1 Mage and 1 Cleric, the five inputs accept Fighter, Fighter, Fighter, Mage and Cleric, from top to bottom. Class indicators update to match your choices. Zero disables a class; advanced classes match their base archetype. The ordinary Adventurer option is not a wildcard.

[b]Send manually or upgrade to automation[/b]
Send now is the default, including when the full party is ready. It can also send an incomplete party. Research Auto Send inside the Party Node tech tree, then purchase/install it through the node's bottom [b]+ upgrade slot[/b], just like upgrades on other nodes. Remove the upgrade to return to manual sending. Research alone does not automate every node.

[b]Enough room for everyone[/b]
Both manual and automatic sending require enough room for the entire batch. A five-member party waits when only four spaces are free. Space is checked again at transfer, so competing arrivals cannot split the party. Stockpiles, inns, quest boards and training grounds count their occupied internal spaces and queued arrivals. Dungeon and Party receivers use compatible free adventurer inputs through one party connection; inputs wired to other sources are excluded. Other receivers need room in the connected input itself. No dungeon party-size limits are changed.

[b]A living meetup room[/b]
Only the adventurers actually waiting in the node appear walking around the map table. Their original items, equipment and quality are preserved. The gear keeps configuration out of the workspace. Green arrows are inputs; the blue arrow is the output.

[b]Unlock[/b]
Research Party Node beside Adventurers' Guild in the main tech tree, then build it from the native build menu. Auto Send is researched inside Party Node's own tech tree.

[b]Requirements[/b]
Kingdom .Inc, Windows x64 Unity Mono edition, and [url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases]BepInEx 5.x Windows x64[/url], installed separately. Tested with BepInEx 5.4.23.6. Do not use BepInEx 6/IL2CPP for this DLL. No other gameplay mod is required.

[b]Installation[/b]
Close the game. With BepInEx installed, extract the main ZIP into the folder containing KingdomInc.exe, merging the BepInEx folder. The DLL should be at BepInEx/plugins/PartyNode/PartyNode.dll. Restart the game. Manual installation is verified; Vortex deployment is untested.

[b]Updating and uninstalling[/b]
Close the game before replacing the DLL. Saves retain the lineup, original adventurers, pending output and installed upgrade. Earlier enabled Auto Send popup settings migrate to an installed upgrade; older surplus members remain recoverable in batches of at most five. Before uninstalling, send waiting members, remove Party Nodes, save and close the game. Keep the mod installed when loading saves that still contain its nodes. Back up your kingdom before adding or removing mods.

[b]Compatibility and validation[/b]
114 model/runtime/UI checks passed in a disposable diagnostic kingdom with save writes blocked. Sharable Blueprints' native discovery recognizes the custom node. Broad third-party mod compatibility is untested. The optional source archive is for developers and requires local game/BepInEx references to build.
